How big is this preview catalog?
This GitHub Pages mirror currently surfaces 190 venues and 99 events sampled from the live poolparty.com catalog (Sep 18, 2026 refresh). 22 upcoming dated products this week are Vegas-heavy — other cities keep past-catalog rows or venue cards without inventing dates.
What is PoolParty.com?
PoolParty.com is a discovery + booking surface for pool, beach, yacht, and dayclub parties worldwide. Search by city, browse venues and events, then Book Now into the live ticketing path.
How does Book Now work in September 2026?
Book Now is a live handoff, not a mystery checkout. If the event uses Booketing, the button opens a Booketing microsite. Otherwise it continues on poolparty.com with SquadUp-powered checkout. This preview labels that path on every card and deep-links to it — it does not embed SquadUp or process payments.
Why do some buttons say “Booketing” and others “live checkout”?
Because the live catalog still mixes providers. We mirror that honesty: Booketing links go to booketing.com; on-site/SquadUp products keep you on poolparty.com. Never assume one overlay for every event.
Do you show real ticket prices here?
No. This Miami Electric preview never invents live ticket prices or inventory. Prices, deposits, and fees appear only on the live Booketing/SquadUp stack.
What’s on the calendar this week (Sep 15–20, 2026)?
Vegas is the heat center in this snapshot — Nightclub Access Passes, Liquid Pool, Marquee, Palm Tree, TAO Beach, OMNIA Dayclub names (Cedric, Don Diablo, Steve Aoki, and more as listed live). Other cities may show 0 upcoming — we don’t backfill empty hubs with Vegas.
Why is Miami / Dubai / Ibiza empty for upcoming?
The public product feed for those cities is between seasons or older-dated in this sample. City hubs say so honestly and still show venues + a live city link. Past-catalog rows stay labeled PAST CATALOG.
Can I reschedule, cancel, or get a refund?
Policies differ by event and ticketing provider. This preview cannot promise refunds. Check the terms on the Booketing microsite or SquadUp checkout when you Book Now on the live site. Treat any preview copy as demo guidance only.
Do I need a deposit?
Sometimes — deposits vary by venue and product. You’ll see that during live booking, not as invented numbers on this static site.
How old do I need to be?
Unless an event notes otherwise, you must be of legal drinking age (typically 21+) to purchase and enter.
Passes vs single events — what’s the difference?
Passes (e.g. Nightclub Access Pass) are dated access products across partner rooms. Single events name the DJ and venue. Both still Book Now into Booketing or SquadUp as labeled.
What should I wear?
Swimwear-friendly outfits are the default for pools and beach clubs — expect to get wet. Check each event/venue for dress code and prohibited items (glass, outside alcohol, etc.).
Do venues offer cabanas or VIP?
Many dayclubs offer cabanas, daybeds, and VIP. Details and pricing live on the booking handoff — not invented here. Demo cart tiers are layout-only.
Is the demo cart a real checkout?
No. It’s a quantity-only shell for a future first-party POS conversation. It never charges cards and never replaces SquadUp/Booketing.

What if it rains or the DJ cancels?
Weather and talent changes follow the live ticketing provider’s terms (Booketing microsite or SquadUp checkout) — not this static preview. Read the refund / rain language before you pay. We don’t promise reschedules here.
