Answers before you dive in

Tickets, age rules, refunds and what to expect on the day.

What is PoolParty.com?
PoolParty.com helps you find and book pool, beach, yacht and dayclub parties worldwide. Search by city, browse venues and events, then tap Get tickets.
How does booking work?
Tap Get tickets on any event. You’ll go straight to our secure ticket partner to pick your tickets and pay.
Where do I see ticket prices?
Prices, deposits and fees show up on the ticket page when you tap Get tickets, so you always see the current price.
What’s on this week?
Vegas is packed this week: Nightclub Access Passes, Liquid Pool, Marquee, Palm Tree, TAO Beach and OMNIA Dayclub, with Cedric, Don Diablo, Steve Aoki and more.
Can I reschedule, cancel or get a refund?
It depends on the event. Check the terms on the ticket page before you pay.
Do I need a deposit?
Sometimes. Deposits vary by venue and event, and you’ll see them on the ticket page.
How old do I need to be?
Unless an event says otherwise, you must be of legal drinking age (usually 21+) to buy tickets and get in.
Do I need ID at the door?
Yes. Bring a valid government ID. Door policy is set by each venue.
Passes vs single events: what’s the difference?
Passes (like the Nightclub Access Pass) get you into several partner venues on a date. Single events are one DJ at one venue.
What should I wear?
Swimwear-friendly outfits are the norm at pools and beach clubs, so expect to get wet. Check each event for dress code and banned items (glass, outside alcohol, etc.).
Do venues offer cabanas or VIP?
Many dayclubs offer cabanas, daybeds and VIP tables. You’ll find options and prices on the ticket page.
Can I book for a group or a bachelor / bachelorette party?
Yes. Look for cabanas, daybeds or VIP on the ticket page, or contact us for bigger groups.
What if it rains or the DJ cancels?
Weather and lineup changes follow the ticket terms. Read the refund and rain policy before you pay.
How can venues partner or advertise?
Head to Advertise · Free offer to tell us about your events and ask about featured spots.
How do I contact support?
Email info@poolparty.com or use the contact page.
What’s the music playing?
Soundtrack beds by Sonic Ear Candy. Tap the ♪ button to play, pause or change the volume. Venues and brands can order custom tracks at SonicEarCandy.com.
