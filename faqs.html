Help

Answers before you dive in

Adapted from live PoolParty FAQ themes for partygoers and venues.

What is PoolParty.com?
PoolParty.com is a discovery and booking surface for epic pool, beach, and yacht parties worldwide — search by city, browse venues and events, then follow booking instructions.
How do I book a pool party event?
Open Events, pick your event, then use Book Now. On the live site that continues on Booketing or a SquadUp checkout overlay. This prototype deep-links to those live paths — it does not process payments.
Do I need to pay a deposit?
Payment policies, including deposits, vary by event and venue. Specific details appear during the live booking process.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Rescheduling and cancellation policies differ by event and venue. Refer to the specific event terms during live booking.
How old do I need to be?
Unless noted, you must be of legal drinking age (typically 21+) to purchase tickets and participate.
Why are some tickets different prices?
Prices vary by venue, talent, and demand — a ranked performer costs more than a local DJ set. This prototype never invents live prices.
What should I wear?
Swimwear is recommended — pool parties can get wet. Check each event for dress code.
Do venues offer cabanas or VIP?
Many venues offer VIP cabanas and bungalows. Details are on the specific event page or via contact.
How can venues list with PoolParty?
Use the Advertise page to inquire about listing. Venue owners can optimize listings, attract the right crowd, and manage discovery.
Affiliate / promoter interest?
If you want to spread the word and earn on tickets, use Contact or Advertise and ask for affiliate onboarding — live promotions team follows up.
Prohibited items?
Commonly: outside food/drinks, glass, illegal substances. Always check venue guidelines.
How do I contact support?
Live: info@poolparty.com or Contact Us. This prototype mailto goes to faccett66@gmail.com for Frank’s review.
