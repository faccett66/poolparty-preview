Nightlife at Hakkasan.

Hakkasan Nightclub at MGM Grand is a multi-level nightlife destination. Its spaces include the Level 3 Lounge, Main Nightclub, Pavilion and Mezzanine, alongside the Hakkasan Grid lighting installation.

Hakkasan belongs in the separate Nightlife collection, not in the pool listings. Use its own partner calendar to review the dates and experiences the venue is offering.

Before you book.

  1. Open the Hakkasan Nightclub partner calendar and choose the date you want.
  2. Read the actual product details, entry requirements, pricing and cancellation terms on the partner site.
  3. Confirm your booking with the venue partner. This guide does not reserve entry or guarantee availability.
