Nightlife at OMNIA.

OMNIA is the nightclub at Caesars Palace. The venue combines a main club, the Heart of OMNIA lounge and a terrace, with a large overhead lighting installation central to its design.

This guide is for OMNIA Nightclub. OMNIA Dayclub and the separately listed Skybar experiences use a different partner venue code and are not mixed into this nightclub guide.

Before you book.

  1. Open the OMNIA Nightclub partner calendar and choose the date you want.
  2. Read the actual product details, entry requirements, pricing and cancellation terms on the partner site.
  3. Confirm your booking with the venue partner. This guide does not reserve entry or guarantee availability.
Is this the right venue calendar?

This guide is for OMNIA Nightclub, not OMNIA Dayclub or a separately offered Skybar experience.

Where do I check prices and availability?

Use this venue’s partner calendar. Availability, fees and booking terms can change; no prices, event dates or packages are advertised in this guide.
