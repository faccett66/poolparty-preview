Nightlife at Marquee.

Marquee Nightclub is the after-dark venue at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas. Its club spaces include the main room, the mixed-format Boombox and the more intimate Library.

The nightclub and Marquee Dayclub share a name, but they are separate experiences. This listing takes you to the nightclub calendar rather than the pool or winter Dome calendar.

Before you book.

  1. Open the Marquee Nightclub partner calendar and choose the date you want.
  2. Read the actual product details, entry requirements, pricing and cancellation terms on the partner site.
  3. Confirm your booking with the venue partner. This guide does not reserve entry or guarantee availability.
Is this the right venue calendar?

This guide is for Marquee Nightclub. Marquee Dayclub and the winter Dome remain separate daytime listings.
