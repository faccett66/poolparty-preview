Nightlife at TAO.

TAO Nightclub brings its Las Vegas club experience to The Venetian. The venue combines a high-energy main room with multiple bars and raised viewing areas.

This is the nightclub, not TAO Beach. Keeping the two listings separate makes it easier to choose between a night out and a poolside visit.

Before you book.

  1. Open the TAO Nightclub partner calendar and choose the date you want.
  2. Read the actual product details, entry requirements, pricing and cancellation terms on the partner site.
  3. Confirm your booking with the venue partner. This guide does not reserve entry or guarantee availability.
Is this the right venue calendar?

This guide opens TAO Nightclub’s calendar, not TAO Beach’s pool and dayclub calendar.
